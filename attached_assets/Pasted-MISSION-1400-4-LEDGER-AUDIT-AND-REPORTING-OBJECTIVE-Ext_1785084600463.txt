MISSION 1400.4 — LEDGER, AUDIT AND REPORTING

OBJECTIVE

Extend the authenticated Alpha Intelligence OS web application with secure,
read-only Ledger, Audit and Reporting workspaces.

This sprint must expose the existing deterministic append-only ledger,
Binance TR reconciliation evidence, safe application audit events and mission
reports through a professional searchable interface.

This sprint must not mutate ledger history.
It must not create, submit, modify or cancel orders.
It must not transfer or withdraw funds.
It must not change leverage, margin mode or position mode.

- current Git history
