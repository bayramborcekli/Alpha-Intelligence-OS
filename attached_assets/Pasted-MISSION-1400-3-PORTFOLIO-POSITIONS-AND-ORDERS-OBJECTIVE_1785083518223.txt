MISSION 1400.3 — PORTFOLIO, POSITIONS AND ORDERS

OBJECTIVE

Extend the authenticated Alpha Intelligence OS web application with secure,
read-only Portfolio, Positions and Orders workspaces.

This sprint must turn the existing navigation placeholders into practical
daily-use screens for monitoring Binance Global Futures and Binance TR assets,
open Futures positions and open Futures orders.

This sprint must not create, submit, modify, cancel or close any order.
It must not transfer or withdraw funds.
It must not change leverage, margin mode or position mode.
